About Elison

A sculptor and visual artist working across resin figurines, oil portraiture, and digital concept art. Each piece is a dialogue between material and imagination — rooted in classical anatomy, reaching toward something new.

Based in Russia, Elison work has been exhibited internationally and collected across three continents. Commissions are accepted by appointment.
